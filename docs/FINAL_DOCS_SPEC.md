# NULL RITE — Final Documentation System

Status: **LOCKED FOR PUBLIC DOCS**

This file records the public-facing system decisions approved for NULL RITE. Gate-specific numbers remain intentionally configurable and must be disclosed before each Gate opens.

## Core identity

- Collection: **3232 Vessels** on Robinhood Chain.
- A Vessel is the persistent identity that walks the NULL RITE path.
- $RITE is the means to act: ritual fuel for eligibility, Offerings, decision sealing, specialized access and future interactions.
- Minting / The Summoning uses ETH.
- Artwork is born once; later progression lives in the Vessel's Record.

## Reveal

- Token IDs remain sequential.
- Final artwork, traits and rarity are randomized at Reveal.
- The full 3232-item reveal set is frozen first.
- A provenance hash is published before the reveal seed is knowable.
- The seed source is declared before its value exists.
- Final assignment is deterministic from the public commitment + seed and is independently verifiable.

## The Gate

- The Gate presents paths, conditions and irreversible decisions.
- Previous Record state may change what future Gates reveal.
- Exact Gate content is not spoiled in advance.
- Public docs explain the mechanics while preserving hidden choices, conditions and outcomes.

## The Offering

- Certain Gate actions require an Offering in $RITE.
- Accepted Offerings are permanently burned.
- Offering amounts are Gate-specific and are not fixed permanently in the core Docs.
- An Offering does not purchase a guaranteed outcome.

## Main Gates and Resonance Pools

- **Every Main Gate carries a Resonance Pool.**
- The Pool, funding reference, completion requirements and distribution rules are disclosed before the Gate opens.
- A Pool may contain $RITE, ETH, Relics, access, collaborator assets or other specifically announced rewards.
- The Pool is funded separately before entry.
- Accepted Offerings do **not** fund the reward promised by that same Gate.
- Hidden / side rituals are not required to carry a Resonance Pool.

## Completion and distribution

- Entering is not enough; completion earns Pool eligibility.
- Completion establishes base eligibility.
- Main Gates may use bounded Resonance Weight modifiers based on paths, Marks or completion states.
- Exact weights are published before entry.
- Main Gates may use a reward-bearing Vessel cap per wallet to reduce concentrated Pool capture.
- The cap is published before entry and does not prevent extra Vessels from building their Record.

## Ownership rules

- Reward entitlement already earned from a completed Gate belongs to the **wallet that completed it**.
- Gate history belongs to the **Vessel**.
- Transferring a Vessel does not transfer old unclaimed reward entitlement unless a future Gate explicitly states otherwise.
- The Vessel's persistent Record remains attached after transfer.

## Historical rarity

- Born rarity is revealed.
- Historical rarity is earned.
- Marks, path history, classifications, completed rituals and other persistent states can differentiate Vessels over time.

## Relics

- Relics are an official system feature.
- Relics are rare Vessel-bound artifacts associated with specific paths, hidden conditions or exceptional completion states.
- Relics are not guaranteed to every participant.
- A Relic may function as proof, a future access key, a ritual condition or another disclosed function.
- Vessel-bound Relics remain with the Vessel when ownership changes.

## Ritual sequence

1. $RITE awakens.
2. 3232 Vessels are summoned.
3. The Assembly forms onchain.
4. Born artwork, traits and rarity are revealed.
5. Resonance begins.
6. The Gate opens.
7. Certain actions require an Offering and burn $RITE.
8. Completion earns Resonance Pool eligibility.
9. The Record expands and influences what comes next.

## Core public lines

> A Vessel is the identity. $RITE is the means to act.

> The Gate doesn't give you something. The Gate makes you choose something.

> Every choice changes what the next Gate shows you.

> Born rarity is revealed. Historical rarity is earned.

> The Offering is burned. The completion is rewarded. The history remains.

## Gate-specific parameters

The following are intentionally **not permanently fixed** in the core Docs and must instead be disclosed before participation in each Gate:

- Offering amount;
- Pool size and assets;
- Gate duration;
- completion requirements;
- Resonance Weight values;
- reward-bearing Vessel cap per wallet;
- distribution formula;
- hidden / side ritual-specific rules.
